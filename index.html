FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CashbackMantra AI. Can't find an answer? Contact us.

What is CashbackMantra AI?
CashbackMantra AI is an AI-powered shopping intelligence platform. Instead of showing static coupon lists, it analyzes prices, cashback, coupons, bank offers, credit card rewards and price history across major e-commerce stores to tell you exactly when and where to buy for maximum savings.
How does AI cashback optimization work?
Our Cashback Intelligence engine evaluates every available offer — store cashback, coupon stacking, bank promotions and card reward programs — then calculates the combination that produces the highest effective savings for your specific purchase and payment methods.
Is the platform free?
Yes. The Free plan includes basic AI searches, coupon discovery and cashback comparison forever. Pro unlocks unlimited searches, price predictions and smart alerts, while Enterprise adds analytics, API access and team accounts.
How accurate are price predictions?
Predictions use historical price data, seasonality and market signals to forecast likely price movements, and each prediction includes a confidence score. While no forecast is guaranteed, our models are designed to identify the most probable buying window.
Which stores are supported?
We support 120+ major Indian e-commerce platforms including Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Croma, AJIO, Nykaa and more. We continually expand coverage based on user demand.
How are coupon codes verified?
Our Smart Coupon Verification engine ranks and validates coupons using success-rate signals and AI analysis instead of showing random, expired codes — so you see the codes most likely to actually work.
How do deal alerts work?
Set a target price for any product and we'll notify you the moment it's reached. Our Smart Deal Alerts only trigger when a deal is genuinely worth considering, so you avoid notification fatigue.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We follow privacy-first principles, use encryption in transit and at rest, and never sell your personal data. You control your information and can export or delete it at any time. See our Privacy Policy.
Can I use multiple payment methods?
Absolutely. Add your preferred credit and debit cards to your Smart Wallet, and the AI Credit Card Optimizer will recommend the best card to maximize effective cashback and rewards for each purchase.
How do I contact support?
Email us anytime at hello@cashbackmantra.in or use the form on our Contact page. We aim to respond within one business day.
Does CashbackMantra AI sell products directly?
No. We are an independent shopping intelligence platform. We help you decide where, when and how to buy — you complete your purchase on the retailer's own website.
Is CashbackMantra AI affiliated with the retailers it analyzes?
CashbackMantra AI is an independent platform and is not affiliated with any retailer unless explicitly stated. All store names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
